Two blocking bugs found in playtest of v27, please fix exactly these and nothing else (do not post an update). (1) NEW GAME LOOP: with no save, the title shows only 'Новая игра'; it asks 'Начать заново?', clears the save and reloads — the page comes back to the same title, so a new player can never start. Fix in renderTitleMenu: document.querySelector('#new-game').onclick = hasSave ? askNewGame : begin. (2) OLIVER CANNOT SCORE: in shootMatch the ball is placed at the shooter's position with no pickup delay, so the shooter re-grabs it on the next frame; and while the shot is flying the keeper picks it up by proximity, which ignores the computed save chance. Fix: in shootMatch replace 'ball.owner=null;ball.x=p.x;' with 'ball.owner=null;ball.ignoreOwnerUntil=m.elapsed+.4;ball.x=p.x+direction*8;'; in the loose-ball pickup condition add '&&!(ball.wasShot&&Math.hypot(ball.vx,ball.vy)>60)' so a fast shot in flight cannot be grabbed (the save is already decided in shootMatch). Then rebalance FINAL_CONFIG: keeperSaveBase .6, keeperSaveMin .4, aiShotInterval 5. Tested locally with these exact changes: a perfect team where Oliver shoots scores about 4 of 10 close shots, a weak team loses about 0:5, and the ending sequence works.
